Research Overview

Annual indicators describe overall energy and carbon performance, but can conceal the hourly mismatch between building demand and PV generation. Arch-EPVC connects both scales to support school-building design assessment and photovoltaic potential evaluation.

Multimodal Building Data

OBJ geometry, building attributes, envelope properties, weather time series and simulated performance are linked through building identifiers for teaching buildings in Shandong Province, China.

Dual-Scale Prediction

The annual route combines geometry and numerical features. The hourly route additionally incorporates weather and temporal inputs to represent energy demand, PV generation and operational carbon emissions.

New Design & Retrofit

The research supports early-stage comparison of new school designs and performance/PV-potential assessment of existing teaching buildings, within the dataset coverage and simulation assumptions.
