Arch-EPVC: School Building Energy, PV and Carbon Prediction

A multimodal temporal framework for predicting school-building energy use, photovoltaic generation and operational carbon emissions from 3D geometry, structured design variables and weather time series.

Open Arch-EPVC Platform ↗Explore the research

Why this project?

School buildings have intensive daytime use and considerable photovoltaic potential. Annual indicators alone can hide the hourly mismatch between building demand and PV generation. This project connects annual performance prediction with hourly energy, PV and carbon assessment.

The framework supports early-stage evaluation of new school designs and performance or PV-retrofit assessment of existing teaching buildings, within the coverage of the research data and simulation assumptions.

Research foundation

A multimodal building dataset

The study brings together building geometry, structured attributes and environmental time series to examine school-building performance at complementary temporal scales.

Building identifiers connect geometry, numerical records and hourly outputs.

  • 3D OBJ massing models and geometry-derived attributes.
  • Building properties and envelope thermal parameters.
  • Regional weather conditions and temporal information.
  • Annual energy, PV, carbon and derived indicators.
  • Hourly simulated energy demand, PV generation and operational carbon data.

Dual-scale prediction

Geometry, attributes and time

The annual branch fuses three-dimensional geometry and numerical building features. The hourly branch additionally incorporates weather and time-series inputs to represent dynamic energy and photovoltaic behaviour.

  • 3D CNN for building morphology.
  • TabTransformer for structured attributes.
  • LSTM for temporal relationships.
